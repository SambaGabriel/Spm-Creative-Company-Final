Privacy Policy

Effective June 9, 2026

SPM Creative Company USA LLC ("SPM", "we", "us") operates spmcreativecompany.com. This policy explains, in plain language, what information this website handles and what we do with it.

What we collect

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to send us through the Artist Vault submission form: your name, your email address, a link to your work, and an optional message. We also receive whatever you send us directly by email.

How we use it

Submissions are delivered to our inbox so our team can review your work and reply between Miami and São Paulo. We use your information only to evaluate and respond to your submission. We do not add you to mailing lists without asking.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not run advertising on this site.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking cookies.

Service providers

Like most websites, we rely on a few services to operate. They may process technical data (such as your IP address) as part of serving the site:

  • Web3Forms delivers the submission form to our inbox.
  • Cloudflare hosts and serves the website.
  • Google Maps renders the location map, and Google Fonts serves the typography.

Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Submissions live in our email inbox for as long as we need them to evaluate your work and maintain our correspondence. If you ask us to delete a submission, we will.

Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask us to access, correct, delete, or hand over the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email contact@spmcreativecompany.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and will never treat you differently for exercising your rights.

  • California residents: you may request disclosure, deletion, and correction of your personal information under California law. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.
  • Brazil: if you are in Brazil, you have the rights provided by the LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados), including confirmation of processing, access, correction, and deletion. Our processing of submission data is based on your consent, given when you submit the form.
  • European Economic Area and United Kingdom: you have the rights provided by the GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Where data is processed

We are a United States company and the services we use process data in the United States. By submitting the form you understand that your information will be processed there.

Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards to protect the little data we handle. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security, but we keep our footprint deliberately small: no databases of visitor data, no tracking, no ad profiles.

Do Not Track

This site does not track visitors across the web, so it behaves the same whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
